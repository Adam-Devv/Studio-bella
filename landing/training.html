Program Overview

Training at Studio Bella Permanent Makeup by Cookie is designed for individuals who want true mastery in permanent makeup and paramedical tattooing—not just a basic introduction. This program combines technical instruction, hands-on experience, and real-world mentorship to prepare students for immediate professional practice.

Students leave with hands-on experience working with live models, a complete professional starter kit, a comprehensive training binder, real-world consultation skills, and the confidence to begin working professionally.

Training Format Options

10-Day Advanced Program

Training days do not need to be consecutive but must be completed within 21 days.

5-Day Intensive Program

Five consecutive training days. Scheduling options may include Monday–Friday, Friday–Tuesday, Saturday–Wednesday, or two and a half consecutive weekends.
